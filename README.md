## Reflection

I've been skeptical about my major in computer science for a while, worrying that by the time I graduate, my potential job will have been taken by AI. This article helped me understand how AI is a tool that speeds up the development process, but we still have a role to play in the process. Based on this information, I'm going to focus on building skills beyond being a good coder. I feel like in the future there will be much less jobs available, and instead of looking for a stable job the right path may be to utilize AI to create my own product, and if this is the case it's best to begin familiarizing myself with AI sooner rather than later.
